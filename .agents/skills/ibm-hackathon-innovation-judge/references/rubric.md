# Innovation rubric — 20 points

| Dimension | Max | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Problem reframing | 5 | Exposes a consequential blind spot in how work is currently done and makes it easy to understand. |
| Differentiation | 5 | Names credible alternatives/baselines and demonstrates a meaningful workflow or outcome advantage. |
| Technical novelty | 4 | Implements a nontrivial mechanism or composition that is central to the result. |
| Defensibility and learning | 3 | Evaluation data, feedback loops, provenance, or accumulated evidence makes the solution improve or stay trustworthy. |
| Demo aha moment | 3 | A short demo visibly turns an overlooked risk or costly task into an actionable decision. |

Apply the lowest relevant cap:

- Generic AI wrapper with no differentiated workflow: 8/20.
- Differentiation asserted without baseline or competitor evidence: 15/20.
- Novelty exists only in the pitch, not the artifact: 12/20.

Do not confuse more features with innovation, a graph UI with a novel decision workflow, a new prompt with defensible technical novelty, or synthetic examples with market validation.
