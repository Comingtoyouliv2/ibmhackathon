# Challenge Fit rubric — 20 points

| Dimension | Max | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Future of Work outcome | 5 | Directly improves how a named individual, team, or organization achieves a work outcome. |
| AI as collaborator | 5 | AI interprets context and helps plan, coordinate, decide, or execute with inspectable reasoning/evidence. |
| Automation, decision quality, and speed | 4 | The flow visibly removes repetitive steps, improves a decision, or shortens time-to-outcome. |
| Workflow integration | 3 | Shows where the system enters and exits a real workflow, including handoff or action. |
| IBM Bob and program alignment | 3 | Bob has implemented, visible value in the judged flow and is described accurately. |

Apply the lowest relevant cap:

- Generic AI with no concrete work outcome: 10/20.
- No real workflow entry/exit is shown: 14/20.
- IBM Bob is only branding or future intent: 17/20.

The strongest pitch answers: “Who was doing what, what decision was missed, what does the AI now do, and what happens next?”
