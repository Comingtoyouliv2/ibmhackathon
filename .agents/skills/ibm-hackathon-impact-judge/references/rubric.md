# Real-World Impact rubric — 20 points

| Dimension | Max | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Pain and stakes | 4 | Shows a frequent or costly work failure and its consequence with credible evidence. |
| Target-user specificity | 3 | Defines a beachhead user, context, current behavior, and decision owner. |
| Measurable outcome definition | 4 | Defines baseline and target for time, errors, review load, incidents, or another user outcome. |
| Real-world validation | 4 | Real repositories, users, pilots, or deployments demonstrate the outcome with limitations disclosed. |
| Scale, reach, and accessibility | 3 | Generalization path and access constraints are credible; scale does not erase trust. |
| Adoption and ROI story | 2 | Benefit exceeds switching, false-positive, compute, privacy, and maintenance costs. |

Apply the lowest relevant cap:

- No target user and baseline workflow: 9/20.
- No real repository, user, or outcome validation: 13/20.
- Impact only expressed as potential with no measurable outcome: 14/20.
- Claims materially exceed evidence: 10/20.

Real OSS scans demonstrate environmental relevance. They do not by themselves demonstrate saved review time, prevented regressions, or adoption.
