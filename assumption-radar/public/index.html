How it works

Four steps from open-PR chaos to a short, explained conflict list.

Step 1

Connect your repo

Paste a GitHub repo URL and a read-only token. We fetch all open PRs and their diffs — nothing is installed in your CI.

Step 2

IBM Bob extracts intent

Each PR gets an Intent Card — what it tries to do, which functions it touches, and what it assumes about the rest of the code.

Step 3

Semantic bucketing

PRs are clustered by functional area, cutting n² comparisons down to a few dozen candidate pairs worth judging.

Step 4

Conflict judgment

IBM Bob reasons over each pair: severity, confidence, and a plain-English explanation of the conflict — plus a suggested fix.
